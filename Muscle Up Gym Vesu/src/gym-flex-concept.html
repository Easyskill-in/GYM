About Gym Flex

More than a gym.
A floor built for progress.

Gym Flex sits on the upper floor of V3 Corner, a few steps from L.P. Savani School. Members consistently describe it the same way in public reviews: spacious, well kept, and stocked with equipment you can actually train on — not a crowded room where you queue for a bench.

01

Space to train properly

Room for compound lifts, warm-ups and stretching without working around someone else's set.

02

Equipment that holds up

Free weights, plate-loaded machines and cardio maintained well enough that reviewers mention it by name.

03

Timings that fit your day

Doors open from 6 AM through 10 PM, so early shifts and late finishes both work.

How it starts

Your transformation
starts on day one.

Nobody walks in already knowing what to do. Here's how a first month at Gym Flex is designed to go.

  1. Step 1

    Free trial session

    Walk the floor, try the equipment, ask anything. No commitment.

  2. Step 2

    Goal and fitness check

    A short assessment so training starts from where you actually are.

  3. Step 3

    Your programme

    A written plan for the week, matched to your schedule and experience.

  4. Step 4

    Track and adjust

    Regular check-ins so the plan moves as fast as you do.
